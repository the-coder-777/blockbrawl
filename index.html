Controls, hotbar & tips

Hotbar layout: put any item in any slot (saved on this device)

WASD move · Space jump (twice for a double jump) · Shift sprint · Ctrl or C crouch · E dash
Left click use item · 1 sword · 2 bow (hold to charge) · 3 blocks · 4 pickaxe · 5 wind charge
Right click place a block · Tab scores · T chat · Esc release the mouse
Blocks you place crumble after a minute. Lava burns, and falling off the edge counts as a death. Connections run browser-to-browser, so if the room's creator closes their tab the room ends.
